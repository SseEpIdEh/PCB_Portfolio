# Personal Portfolio Website

This repository contains my personal portfolio website. The portfolio highlights my academic background, research interests, engineering projects, PCB design work, microfluidic lab-on-a-chip systems, machine learning projects, and technical reports.

## About Me

I am Sepideh Mohammadi, a PhD student in Computer Science with a background in Electrical Engineering. My research focuses on microfluidic lab-on-a-chip systems, digital microfluidics, PCB design, embedded electronics, and hardware-software integration for biomedical applications.

I am also interested in robotics and Physical AI, especially systems that connect sensing, control, computation, and real-world physical interaction.

## Portfolio Content

This portfolio includes:

- PCB design projects
- Microfluidic and lab-on-a-chip research
- Digital microfluidics and EWOD-related work
- Embedded electronics and hardware testing
- Machine learning projects
- Robotics and Physical AI interests
- Technical reports and project documentation

## Project Purpose

This portfolio was created to present my research, technical skills, and hands-on engineering experience in one place. It also shows how my project-based coursework helped me improve my PCB design skills, work on real-world engineering problems, and connect my technical background to my current research.
